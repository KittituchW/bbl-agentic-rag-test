International Travel Policy: Employees traveling abroad for business must obtain written approval from their department head at least 14 days before departure. The company covers economy-class airfare, accommodation up to 4,500 THB per night, and a daily meal allowance of 1,200 THB. Employees must purchase travel insurance through the approved corporate provider and register their itinerary with the HR portal. Travel to countries under a government-issued travel advisory requires additional approval from the Chief Risk Officer.

Domestic Travel Policy: Business trips within Thailand require approval from an immediate supervisor at least 3 working days in advance. Employees may claim mileage at 6 THB per kilometer for personal vehicle use, or book standard-class train and bus tickets through the corporate booking system. Hotel reimbursement is capped at 2,000 THB per night for domestic stays.

Expense Reimbursement Policy: All expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense date, accompanied by original receipts or electronic tax invoices. Claims above 10,000 THB require pre-approval from a department head. Reimbursements are processed with the monthly payroll cycle. Alcohol, personal entertainment, and traffic fines are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Remote Work Policy: Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with supervisor approval, subject to team scheduling needs. Remote workers must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00, use the company VPN for all work systems, and attend the weekly team meeting in person. Fully remote arrangements are considered only for exceptional circumstances and require Human Resources sign-off.

Annual Leave Policy: Full-time employees receive 12 days of annual leave per year, increasing to 15 days after 5 years of service. Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. A maximum of 5 unused leave days may be carried over to the following year and must be used before the end of March.

Information Security Policy: Employees must use multi-factor authentication on all company accounts and lock their workstations when unattended. Company data may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage services. Suspected phishing emails must be reported to the IT Security team within 24 hours. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 180 days.

Procurement Policy: Purchases above 50,000 THB require three competitive quotations and approval from the Procurement Committee. Purchases below this threshold require only department head approval. All vendors must be registered in the approved supplier list before a purchase order can be issued. Splitting purchases to avoid approval thresholds is strictly prohibited.

Code of Conduct: Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, and avoid conflicts of interest. Gifts from vendors or clients exceeding 3,000 THB in value must be declared to the Compliance department. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation against whistleblowers are grounds for immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

Corporate Card Policy: Employees issued corporate cards may use them only for approved business expenses. Cardholders must upload receipts to the finance portal within 7 calendar days of each transaction. Cash withdrawals are prohibited unless pre-approved by the Chief Financial Officer. Lost or stolen cards must be reported to Finance Operations and the issuing bank within 24 hours. Monthly statements must be reconciled by the 5th working day of the following month.

Customer Data Privacy Policy: Customer personal data may be accessed only for an assigned business purpose. Files containing national ID numbers, account numbers, phone numbers, addresses, or other personally identifiable information must be encrypted before internal sharing. Data exports to external parties require approval from the Data Protection Officer and a signed data-sharing agreement. Customer data must not be sent to personal email accounts or consumer messaging applications.

Incident Response Policy: Security incidents such as malware infection, lost devices, unauthorized account access, or accidental data disclosure must be reported to the IT Security team within 2 hours of discovery. Employees should preserve evidence, and avoid deleting suspicious files. Critical incidents require notification to Legal, Compliance, and the Chief Information Security Officer.

Vendor Onboarding Policy: New vendors must complete due diligence before a contract or purchase order is issued. Required checks include sanctions screening, tax registration verification, conflict-of-interest declaration, and information security review for technology vendors. High-risk vendors require Compliance approval and annual reassessment. Contracts may not begin until the vendor record is active in the approved supplier system.

Training and Certification Policy: Employees assigned to regulated roles must complete mandatory compliance training within 30 days of appointment and renew it annually. Technical staff may request reimbursement for role-related professional certifications up to 25,000 THB per calendar year with manager pre-approval, proof of passing, and continued employment for 6 months after reimbursement.

Office Equipment Policy: Company laptops, access badges, security tokens, and mobile devices remain company property. Employees must not lend company equipment to other people or install unapproved software. Damaged or lost equipment must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 working day. On separation from the company, all assigned equipment must be returned before the final payroll cycle.
